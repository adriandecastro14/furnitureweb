Our Story

Furniture that feels like home

We started Narra & Co. because we kept walking into showrooms full of furniture that looked beautiful and felt like nobody lived there. So we make the other kind — pieces with soft edges, honest materials, and colours that warm up a room the moment they arrive.

Everything is designed here and built with local craftspeople, in small runs. That means we know who made your sofa, and we can tell you when it will land at your door.

  • Made locally — designed and built in the Philippines
  • Four colourways — Terracotta, Sage, Navy, Mustard
  • Small batches — no warehouse full of the same chair
